**Case simulation: “Coffee deal” between old classmates**

In early April 2026, Li Qiang (Party B), working hard in Shanghai, could not sleep for worry over renovation costs for his “Time Imprint” café. On April 10 he met old classmate Zhang Hua (Party A) at a small restaurant in Shanghai’s Jing’an District.

Li Qiang opened with: “Lao Zhang, the shop still needs RMB 100,000 for an espresso machine, plus RMB 50,000 for green-bean stock—a RMB 150,000 hole. If you can swing it, help a brother out.”

Zhang Hua nodded: “Done—we go way back. I’ll wire the full RMB 150,000 to you today, April 10. But even brothers settle accounts: let’s use a ‘one-fen’ rate—OK?”

Li Qiang thought it over: “OK! For that rate I’ll WeChat you last month’s interest before the 10th each month. On principal, give me eighteen months: on October 9, 2027 I’ll pay the final interest installment together with the RMB 150,000 principal in one lump sum.”

Zhang Hua took a drag: “Fine—but if it goes bad: if you’re late, interest keeps running plus an extra RMB 5 per RMB 10,000 per day as penalty—‘five per ten-thousand’ a day. And this money is only for the café’s purchases—no stocks or plugging other holes.”

On identity: Li Qiang handed over ID showing address № 100 Qiancheng Road, Pudong New Area, Shanghai (number 310101199202026666). Zhang Hua produced a copy from his briefcase: Zhang Hua, № 8 Yard Yangguang Avenue, Chaoyang District, Beijing (number 110101199001018888).

Zhang Hua added: “We’d better say it in the contract—if we ever fight in court I won’t shuttle both ends. Even though we’re meeting in Shanghai, if there’s a dispute it gets heard at the Chaoyang District court by my place in Beijing.”

Just as they settled the RMB 150,000 loan, Zhang Hua (Party A) stayed cautious. He knew Li Qiang (Party B) had married Wang Mei two years earlier (ID number 310101199303037777).

Zhang Hua asked: “Qiangzi, this money’s for your shop—does your wife Wang Mei know? Is she on board?”

Li Qiang slapped his chest: “Of course! This shop is how our family plans to get by—she’s all for it. She even chose the renovation plan. This is absolutely a big family decision for us.”
