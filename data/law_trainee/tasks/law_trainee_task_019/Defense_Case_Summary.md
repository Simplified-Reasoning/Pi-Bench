Case summary

Prosecuting authority: Shuigong District People’s Procuratorate, Jiangzhou City
Defendant: A
Charge: Offense of causing a traffic accident

Summary:
On the night of 24 March 2026, at about 22:00, A and B had late-night drinks with others at a barbecue shop in Datan Village; four people consumed one pitcher of beer, three liters per pitcher. At about 02:00 on 25 March, A drove his own electric motorcycle and gave B a lift toward Xiaotan. At about 02:30, while traveling down the slope in front of Xiaotan High School, A—riding too fast after drinking—struck the cargo box of a small truck parked in violation of rules at the roadside, causing both A and B to fall. After getting up, A saw no obvious wounds on B, who was lying on the ground snoring; A attributed this to intoxication and did not take B to a hospital. Because A did not know B’s address, A and an online acquaintance C together took B to a rented room, Unit 208, roughly 200 meters from the scene; check-in was at about 03:00 on 25 March. After completing check-in and settling B, at about 03:30 A and C were about to leave when the landlord D urged them to have one person stay to watch B; A and C gave no clear answer and left. At about 11:00 on 25 March, D found B in an abnormal state and called the police. A doctor from Xiaotan Hospital arrived and confirmed B was deceased. Forensic examination found B’s death consistent with severe craniocerebral injury from blunt force to the head.

Scene examination:
1. In Room 208, bloodstains measuring approximately 9 cm × 23 cm were found on the wooden bed pillow; a towel with bloodstains at the head of the bed; on the side of the bed near the door, a pair of white socks and a pair of intact black athletic shoes.
2. On the illegally parked small truck, two slight scrapes on the left rear corner of the body. At the roadside beside the truck, one scrape mark; no motorcycle braking marks and no bloodstains.
3. The motorcycle involved showed slight indentation and scrapes at the front, the right rear-view mirror broken off, and deformation of the right bumper.

Police interview of C:
At about 03:00 on 25 March 2026, I got a call from my friend A asking whether I knew a place nearby to rent a room and to help open one. About twenty minutes later I reached the crash scene, and together with A I slowly helped B to the inn. I helped B check in, then A, I, and the landlord took B to the room, removed shoes and socks, covered him with a blanket, and applied a warm towel to his forehead; then A and I left. That is what happened.

Police interview of D:
At about 03:00 on 25 March 2026, I was asleep at the rental property. I am the landlord. Three people came to rent a room—two men and a woman. One man appeared drunk; the woman told me her friend was drunk and needed to stay the night. The woman paid for the room for one night in Unit 208. I helped them carry the drunk man to the room to sleep. After a while everyone except the drunk man came downstairs. I told them they had better leave someone to watch the drunk man. They did not agree and left. At about 11:00 on 25 March I entered Room 208 to remind the guest to check out, saw the man’s face was dark and he did not respond; I felt his hand was still warm, called emergency services, then notified the woman who had rented the room. About thirty minutes later the woman from the night before and another man arrived. The ambulance arrived; the doctor examined the drunk man but said he was already dead, then I called the police. That is the course of events.

Police interrogation of A:
At about 22:00 on 24 March 2026, B called and asked me to drink in Datan Village. At about 22:30 B and I met in Datan; it was our first meeting. We drank until past 02:00. I learned B lived in Xiaotan, so I rode my electric motorcycle to take B back toward Xiaotan factory dormitory. On the downward slope in front of Xiaotan High School I saw a small truck parked haphazard ahead; it occupied nearly half the roadway. Because I was going too fast I could only adjust direction but still hit the truck. We both fell. After I got up I called to B but he was groggy and hard to rouse, so I pulled him to the roadside and called C to help rent a room. I saw no bleeding on B and first righted the motorcycle. At about 02:50 C arrived; by then B was lying on the road snoring. C and I first tried to lift B but could not. Then I had C put B’s elbow and shoulder on my shoulder while C supported the other side, and we barely got B walking. C went in to register the room while I waited. After registration, with D’s help we used the same method to carry B to the room. We first put B on the bed; D then left the room while B was still snoring. I asked C to press a warm towel to B’s forehead; after that we closed the door without locking. Because my hand was scraped, C and I went to Xiaotan Hospital for treatment first, then went home to rest. At about 11:00 this morning C got a call from landlord D asking her to come to the inn. When C and I reached Room 208 we found blood from B’s nose and the towel used on his forehead was stained with blood. I touched him and his body was cold. I called emergency services and also looked through B’s phone to notify his friends but none said they knew B. When the doctor arrived B had no heartbeat; the doctor called the police and we waited on scene for the officers. That is the situation.
