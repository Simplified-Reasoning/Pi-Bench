A and B each made paid-in capital contributions at shareholding ratios of 60% and 40%, respectively, and incorporated Company Jia in Shuigong District, Jiangzhou City; Company Jia holds the requisite professional credentials, and B serves as its legal representative. C is the sole investor in Company Yi, located in Shuizhou District, Jiangzhou City.

On December 1, 2025, Company Jia and Company Yi entered into a contract whereby Company Yi agreed to sell to Company Jia four AIUW-model production machine tools, and Company Yi would pay it an amount of RMB 10 million close to actual market value. The next day, Company Jia paid RMB 10 million; Jia and Yi also entered into a contract under which Company Yi would lease from Company Jia the four AIUW-model machine tools from December 2, 2025 to April 2, 2026, with Company Yi paying Company Jia RMB 3 million in rent on the 2nd of each month. Company Bing assumed joint-and-several guarantor liability for this arrangement; the contract bore Company Jia’s official seal, C’s signature, and the signature of Company Bing’s legal representative E, and took effect the same day. Company Jia paid RMB 500,000 the same day as an earnest-money deposit. In addition, Company Jia and F entered into a guarantee contract under which F would secure Company Yi’s rent payment with real property worth RMB 2 million, and mortgage registration was completed on December 8.

On February 6, 2026, to pay year-end bonuses to employees, Company Yi sold the four AIUW-model machine tools together with a batch of AIUV-model nuts to Company Ding for a total price of RMB 12 million; delivery was completed in Shuigong District, Jiangzhou City, on February 10 of the same year. Investigation showed the batch of AIUV-model nuts was priced at RMB 8 million on the market. Company Ding deployed three machine tools on three separate production lines, each line generating RMB 300,000 in economic benefit per day.

On February 13, 2026, while using an AIUW-model machine tool, Company Ding suffered a quality-related equipment failure on one machine, rendering it scrapped and damaging the line and causing downtime until production resumed on February 28 of the same year. Repair costs for the line were RMB 1 million. On March 2, 2026, Company Ding sued Company Yi in the Shuigong District People’s Court of Jiangzhou City, where Ding’s factory is located, asserting liability for non-conforming performance, seeking replacement of the damaged machine and damages of RMB 5.5 million.

On the same day, Company Yi failed to pay rent to Company Jia as agreed. On March 4, Company Jia sent notice requiring Company Yi to pay overdue rent by March 20. On March 18, Company Yi paid Company Jia RMB 1 million. As of April 5, Company Yi still owed RMB 5 million in rent, and Company Jia intends to bring suit.
