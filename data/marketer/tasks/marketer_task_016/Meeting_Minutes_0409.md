# Meeting Transcript: ROG x BTRT Initial Alignment & Strategy Sync
**Date:** April 9, 2026
**Location:** Virtual (WebEx) / Agency HQ (Hong Kong) / ROG (Taipei) / BTRT (Beijing)
**Attendees:** - Alex Vance (Agency Lead Strategist)
- Marcus Vance (ROG Global Marketing Director)
- Dr. Julian Chen (BTRT Innovation & New Ventures Director)
- Sarah Jenkins (Agency Note-taker - Audio Only)

**Alex:** Alright, it looks like everyone is finally in. Sorry for the delay, the WebEx servers were acting up, and traffic from Kowloon was an absolute nightmare today. How is the weather up in Beijing, Dr. Chen?

**Dr. Chen:** Quite smoggy today, Alex. But we are indoors, drinking good Pu'er tea, so we survive. Thank you for organizing this. Our executive board is closely monitoring this project. BTRT has over three centuries of heritage, but as I mentioned in our preliminary email, our core demographic is aging out. We desperately need to penetrate the Gen-Z and millennial tech-savvy cohorts. 

**Marcus:** And that’s exactly why ROG is here, Doc. Good to see you again. My team in Taipei is super hyped about this. We just wrapped the global finals for the Valorant invitational, and the energy was insane. But honestly, our hardware collaborations have gotten stale. Evangelion, Gundam... it’s always the same anime or streetwear playbook. We want something that literally no one sees coming. A traditional Chinese medicine brand crossing over with the Republic of Gamers? That’s going to break the internet.

**Alex:** Exactly the disruptive energy we want. Let’s align on the physical product first. Have we narrowed down the form factor? I know Sarah sent over some mockups of a custom ROG motherboard with gold-foil accents, and a co-branded herbal tea beverage.

**Dr. Chen:** I saw those. I must be very firm here: we cannot simply package our standard herbal medicine pills in an aggressive-looking black box and call it a day. That dilutes our medical dignity. It feels like a gimmick. The board strictly prohibits using our standard pills for this. It must be a completely novel form factor that provides real physiological value to the user while they are at their desks. 

**Marcus:** I’m 100% with you on that. And from the ROG side, if I see one more lazy reskin of a traditional PC chassis, I’m going to lose my mind. Our community will roast us on Reddit if we just slap a BTRT logo on a mid-tower case and charge a $200 premium. It cannot be a traditional chassis. We need a true fusion peripheral. Something they physically interact with during a 12-hour gaming marathon. 

**Alex:** Understood. Moving away from standard pills and traditional chassis entirely. We’ll brief the industrial design team to focus on ergonomic or biometric fusion devices. Let's pivot to the overarching messaging and the campaign slogan. Dr. Chen, you had some thoughts on integrating traditional philosophy?

**Dr. Chen:** Yes. I consulted with our literature department. We believe the core message should reflect the balance of "Qi"—the vital life force. We drafted a beautiful, classical seven-line poem that translates roughly to "When the digital storm rages, the inner sanctuary remains still." We could print this across the packaging.

**Marcus:** Doc, I respect the poetry, but our audience’s attention span on TikTok is about three seconds. A seven-line poem is going to get skipped immediately. Furthermore, ROG is a global brand. We have massive markets in North America, Europe, and Latin America. Localization of a complex poem is a nightmare. I strongly advocate that we keep the slogan purely in English to ensure global uniformity. 

**Alex:** So, a short English tagline? Maybe a full sentence?

**Marcus:** Shorter. It needs to be punchy enough to engrave on an aluminum bezel or display on a tiny dot-matrix LED screen. Let’s establish a hard cap. Maximum six words. Anything longer is just noise. Six words or fewer, English only. 

**Dr. Chen:** If it guarantees better penetration in the Western markets, I am willing to concede the poem. A concise English phrase is acceptable, provided the meaning carries weight.

**Alex:** Perfect. Six words max, English only. I’ll have the copywriters start brainstorming. Now, visual identity. How are we blending a 300-year-old apothecary aesthetic with hardcore gaming?

**Alex:** I was thinking maybe we stick to the classic ROG matte black and crimson red, but introduce BTRT’s signature imperial gold? 

**Marcus:** Too predictable. Gold and red feels like a Lunar New Year special edition. We want this to feel like a sci-fi bio-hack. ROG is famous for our Aura Sync RGB—we literally have 16.8 million colors to play with. But for this specific medical-cyberpunk narrative, we shouldn't use a rainbow. We need a signature hue. I want to lock in 'Cyberpunk Neon Green'. 

**Alex:** Just to clarify, as the primary color? Not just an accent?

**Marcus:** Yes, the absolute primary color tone for all social assets, web design, and the product's lighting zones. Think "toxic wasteland meets ultimate antidote." It creates an immediate visual shock.

**Dr. Chen:** Cyberpunk Neon Green... It is highly unconventional for a health brand. However, there are certain rare medicinal herbs that possess a similar vibrant hue. I will approve this, under the strict condition that the materials used are premium—anodized metals, perhaps—so the green does not end up looking like cheap plastic toys.

**Alex:** Excellent. Cyberpunk Neon Green is locked as the primary visual tone. Finally, let’s discuss the Go-To-Market strategy and the sales mechanism. We need to create a massive splash for the launch.

**Marcus:** This is my favorite part. We use the streetwear drop model. SNKRS app style. We do a blind countdown timer on X. We only produce 1,000 units globally for the first wave. We make them practically impossible to get. The hype drops and flash sales will drive crazy engagement, the servers will crash, and they’ll instantly hit the secondary market at triple the price. We want to trigger extreme FOMO. 

**Dr. Chen:** Marcus, I admire your passion, but I must exercise my veto power right here. 

**Marcus:** Wait, why? It’s guaranteed free PR.

**Dr. Chen:** Because we are fundamentally a health and wellness brand. Our entire corporate philosophy is built around relieving stress, curing ailments, and restoring balance to people's lives. Deliberately inducing anxiety, panic, and FOMO among our customers completely contradicts our brand ethos. We strictly prohibit any form of hunger marketing or flash sales for this collaboration. 

**Alex:** So, an open pre-order system? 

**Dr. Chen:** Exactly. The sales mechanism must guarantee a stable, adequate public supply throughout the campaign period. If a programmer or an e-sports athlete realizes they need this product to improve their physical well-being, they should not have to fight scalper bots to obtain it. We must ensure anyone who wants to buy it, can buy it. 

**Marcus:** ...Alright. It goes against my usual instincts, but I see the logic from a brand-safety perspective. We will do a wide, stable release. But the agency better figure out how to make a non-limited release feel just as exciting.

**Alex:** Leave that to us. Alright, I think we have our foundational pillars. I'll get the team to extract the real constraints from this discussion and draft the kickoff documents. Thanks, everyone.
