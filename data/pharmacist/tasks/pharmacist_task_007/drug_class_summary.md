# Prostate Cancer Drug-Class and Representative-Drug Summary

## ADT

- Functional role: the systemic-treatment backbone that suppresses androgen signaling.
- Common representatives: leuprolide, goserelin, degarelix.
- Common use settings: higher-risk local disease as part of combined treatment, metastatic hormone-sensitive disease, and management under a continuous castration background.

## ARPI

- Functional role: further suppression of the androgen-receptor pathway.
- Common representatives: abiraterone, enzalutamide, apalutamide, darolutamide.
- Common use settings: intensification in metastatic hormone-sensitive disease or systemic treatment in castration-resistant disease.
- ARPI use spans more than one stage, but the exact role still depends on disease state and prior treatment context.

## Chemotherapy

- Functional role: stronger disease control in patients with higher tumor burden, faster progression, or selected castration-resistant settings.
- Common representatives: docetaxel, cabazitaxel.
- Common use settings: high-volume metastatic hormone-sensitive disease or later-line mCRPC treatment.
- Docetaxel and cabazitaxel do not occupy the same point in the pathway.

## Radioligand Therapy

- Functional role: mainly used in selected bone-metastatic settings or specific target-expression backgrounds.
- Common representatives: radium-223, Lu-PSMA-related treatment.
- Common use settings: prominent bone metastasis or a nuclear-medicine-eligible target-expression setting.
- These therapies do not occupy the same broad role as the main pathway backbone.

## Targeted / Precision Therapy

- Functional role: depends on molecular stratification and is not suitable for every patient.
- Common representatives: PARP inhibitors and related targeted options.
- Common use settings: subsets of castration-resistant patients with HRR / BRCA or related alterations.
- Precision treatment depends on subgroup definition rather than all-comers use.

## Use Reminder

- These drug classes are not unconditional substitutes for one another.
- Disease stage, backbone treatment, and later-line changes should be distinguished when mapping these classes.
