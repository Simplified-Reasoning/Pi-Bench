## Inappropriate Flattening To Avoid

1. **Treating all drug classes as parallel substitutes.** ADT, ARPI, chemotherapy, radioligand therapy, and precision therapy are not unconditional substitutes; disease stage, backbone treatment, and later-line sequencing must be distinguished when mapping these classes. [Guideline+Pathway+DrugSummary]
2. **Collapsing localized and metastatic drug discussions.** In localized disease, drugs play an auxiliary role supporting local treatment; the pathway table explicitly warns against mixing this with advanced systemic treatment discussion (药物多为辅助角色，不应和晚期系统治疗混讲). [Guideline+Pathway+DrugSummary]
3. **Ignoring that the same drug class shifts role across stages.** ARPI serves as conditional intensification on top of ADT in mHSPC but becomes a systemic-treatment option under continuous castration in CRPC; its exact role depends on disease state and prior treatment context. [Guideline+DrugSummary]
4. **Failing to distinguish backbone from intensification in mHSPC.** The pathway table specifically flags this (要区分骨架治疗与强化治疗) — ADT is the backbone; ARPI or chemotherapy is the conditional intensification layer, not a universal addition. [Pathway+DrugSummary]
5. **Treating docetaxel and cabazitaxel as interchangeable.** They do not occupy the same point in the pathway — docetaxel is used in high-volume mHSPC or earlier mCRPC, while cabazitaxel is a later-line mCRPC option. [DrugSummary]
6. **Presenting radioligand or precision therapy as all-comers backbone options.** Radioligand therapy applies only in selected bone-metastatic or target-expression settings; precision therapy depends on molecular stratification (HRR/BRCA or related alterations) rather than all-comers use. [DrugSummary]

## Non-Default Intensification Situation

- **mHSPC — when to add ARPI or chemotherapy on top of ADT.** Higher tumor burden, higher progression risk, or a clinical need for more intensive systemic treatment. The guideline and pathway table both indicate that disease burden, symptoms, fitness, and practical treatment goals gate this decision — intensification is not automatic. [Guideline+Pathway]
- **CRPC — when radioligand therapy enters.** Radium-223 or Lu-PSMA-related treatment is appropriate when bone metastasis is prominent or a nuclear-medicine-eligible target-expression setting exists. This is not a general-purpose mCRPC option. [Guideline+DrugSummary]
- **CRPC — when precision therapy enters.** PARP inhibitors apply to castration-resistant patients with confirmed HRR/BRCA or related alterations. Molecular stratification is a prerequisite. [DrugSummary]
- **CRPC — chemotherapy sequencing.** Docetaxel and cabazitaxel occupy different points: cabazitaxel is positioned after docetaxel exposure, not alongside it. [DrugSummary]
- **CRPC — prior treatment exposure affects selection.** The guideline lists prior treatment exposure as a factor in CRPC treatment selection; the drug-class summary notes that ARPI's role depends on disease state and prior treatment context. A patient who already received ARPI in mHSPC faces a different selection landscape in CRPC. [Guideline+DrugSummary]

## Boundary Note

This document summarizes clinical-pathway information for educational purposes and is not individualized treatment advice. Treatment goals differ across stages: localized settings emphasize curative potential, whereas metastatic and castration-resistant settings emphasize delaying progression and controlling symptoms. Drug-class applicability depends on disease stage, prior therapy, and coexisting conditions — drugs should not be treated as one parallel list. All three source documents frame this material as a clinical-pathway summary rather than personal treatment advice. [Guideline+Pathway+DrugSummary]
