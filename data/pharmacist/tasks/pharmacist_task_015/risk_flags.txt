Current risk flags:

1. The API has extremely low water solubility, so precipitation risk is the core first-round problem.
2. If the ethanol ratio is pushed higher, solubility may improve, but irritation risk will also increase.
3. Because the pKa is weakly basic, pH adjustment needs to account for both gel-formation conditions and API stability.
4. Long-term stability, target release rate, and the need for film formation are all still undecided.
5. The current priority is to decide what must be judged first and what can be judged later.
6. If too many route changes are introduced at once, it will become hard to tell whether the first-round problem came from solvent choice, gel base, or pH handling.
