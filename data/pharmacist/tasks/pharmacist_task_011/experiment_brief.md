# CCK-8 Experiment Brief

## Experimental Goal

- Run a first-round dose-exploration experiment to compare the effect of candidate formulation A on MDA-MB-231 cell viability.
- The immediate goal is to see the overall viability trend after 24 h of treatment and then decide whether the concentration gradient needs to be refined later.

## Cell Line and Treatment Conditions

- Cell line: MDA-MB-231
- Plate format: 96-well plate
- Seeding density: about 5000 cells per well
- Attachment time: overnight
- Treatment duration: 24 h

## Dosing Information

- Solvent: DMSO
- The current goal is to keep the final DMSO concentration at or below 0.1%
- Candidate formulation A is currently tested at 5 concentrations: 0.1, 0.3, 1, 3, and 10 uM
- Untreated, solvent-control, and positive-control groups are also included
- In the current pilot layout, 0.1 uM, 0.3 uM, and 1 uM have paired wells, but 3 uM and 10 uM are effectively high-end exploratory points rather than fully balanced duplicate groups.

## Points That Need Special Attention Right Now

- This is only a first-round exploration, so there is no need to make the concentration spacing very dense yet.
- If the plate layout or raw reader values suggest edge effects or abnormal blanks, that should be pointed out first.
- If some conditions need to be tuned again for the cell line or for a pilot experiment, that should also be stated directly.
- The high-end concentrations are not arranged as cleanly as the lower points.
