# Water Bottle Selection Notes

Goal: save one practical water bottle for daily commute plus lab-bench use.

Selection rules:

- Prefer a normal personal-size bottle: about 18-24 oz, or roughly 500-750 mL.
- Prefer leakproof or secure-lid designs because the bottle may be carried in a backpack.
- Prefer non-glass material because it may sit near lab supplies.
- A simple bottle or one-hand lid is acceptable.
- Avoid gallon jugs, oversized gym bottles, fragile glass bottles, and awkward straw-only bottles.
- Add exactly one suitable product to the wish list, not the cart.
