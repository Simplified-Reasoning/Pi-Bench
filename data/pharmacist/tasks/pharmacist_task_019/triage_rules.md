# Cross-Project Procurement Triage Rules

The goal is to merge the two inherited procurement strands into one triage plan, not to buy everything.

This task uses a hard immediate-order cap: exactly 8 items can enter the first purchase layer.

## First Priority

Put qPCR / cell blockers first because they can break the next experiment.

The five required first-layer blockers are:

- target-gene primers
- sealing film
- RNase-free filter tips
- CCK-8 reagent
- 96-well cell plates

Do not promote FBS, Trypsin, or the 0.22 um sterile filter into the first eight slots unless confirmation changes the risk ranking.

## Gel Pilot Priority

After the five qPCR / cell blockers, only the three-item carbomer-baseline core fits the eight-slot cap:

- Carbomer 940
- Triethanolamine
- Propylene Glycol

The following remain useful, but should move to a next-wave gel-basics bucket rather than the immediate 8-slot order:

- Glycerin
- EDTA / Citric Acid
- 0.22 um filter
- Spatula and Ointment Jar

The 0.22 um filter appears in both gel and general lab support contexts, so do not duplicate it as two immediate buys.

## Do Not Put In Immediate Buy-Now Bucket

Keep route-tied or final-package decisions out of the immediate bucket unless confirmation changes the plan:

- Aluminum Tube
- Airless Pump Bottle
- preservative system / Phenoxyethanol
- Hydroxyethyl Cellulose or other alternative gel base

## Bad Draft Patterns To Correct

A weak merged plan will:

- order packaging first
- merge every item into one universal purchase bucket
- treat preservative as automatically buy-now
- treat FBS, Trypsin, or either 0.22 um filter line as immediate despite the slot cap
- let qPCR / cell blockers wait behind gel-side items
- leave deferred items empty
- leave no separate confirmation bucket
- wait for a reply before ordering everything
